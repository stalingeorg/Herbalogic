Our Roots & Inspiration

Ayurvedic Heritage from Kerala

Ashvana® is inspired by a long-standing family connection with traditional Ayurvedic knowledge in Kerala and by the classical concept of Medhya Rasayana—the revered Ayurvedic botanical tradition associated with plants historically cultivated in relation to mindful balance and clarity.

The name Ashvana draws inspiration from the Ashvins, the revered figures of Vedic antiquity associated with wellbeing, equilibrium, and nature's restorative cycles.

Reflecting approximately four years of formulation refinement and botanical research, Ashvana bridges time-honoured botanical heritage with modern nutritional science, prepared respectfully for contemporary British lifestyles.
